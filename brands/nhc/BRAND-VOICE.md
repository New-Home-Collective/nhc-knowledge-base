## Universal Writing Rules

These apply to every word NHC publishes. No exceptions.

- **No em dashes.** Use a period or rewrite the sentence.
- **Fifth-grade reading level.** Short sentences. Common words.
- **Short paragraphs.** Two to four sentences max. One idea per paragraph.
- **Punchy line breaks.** Let lines breathe.
- **Contractions.** Use them naturally.
- **Conversational.** Write how Bob talks, not how a lawyer writes.
- **Storytelling over polish.** A real moment beats a perfect sentence.
- **Single-word lines for emphasis** when the moment calls for it.
- **Active voice.** Always.
- **"You" and "your."** Speak directly to the reader.
- **No jargon** without a plain-language explanation.
- **Reading level check:** if a 10-year-old would struggle, rewrite it.

## Tone by Content Type

- **Buyer content:** Helpful guide. Reassuring. Step by step.
- **Seller content:** Confident. Results-driven. Data-backed.
- **Cash offer content:** Clear, direct, no pressure, honest.
- **Agent recruiting content:** Inspiring and real. No cheesy MLM energy.
- **Market update content:** Authoritative. Data-forward. Plain language.
- **Local and community content:** First person. Specific. Enthusiastic without being fake.
- **Listing content:** Factual. Only describe what is verifiable.

## Phrases to Avoid

- "In this blog post we will..." (generic opener)
- "At New Home Collective, we strive to..." (corporate)
- "Premier," "luxury," "elite" (overused in real estate)
- "Just wanted to reach out," "hope this finds you well" (dead email openers)
- Anything that reads like a press release
- Vague claims with no numbers behind them
- Any retired tagline

## Fair Housing Rules (mandatory, every skill, every time)

Never include language that references, implies, or describes the people who live in an area by:

- Race, color, or national origin
- Religion
- Sex, gender identity, or sexual orientation
- Familial status (including "great for families" as a standalone descriptor)
- Disability

**Do describe:** walkability, school proximity, commute times, home sizes, price ranges, parks, restaurants, median prices. Let the data speak.

**Say:** "near top-rated schools"
**Not:** "great school district for your kids"

Never describe who would or would not fit in a home or neighborhood.

## Client Philosophy

Every piece of content should do at least one of these:

- Create clarity
- Build confidence
- Reduce stress
- Provide a clear next step

Focus on the client's goals, timeline, financial outcome, and convenience. The highest price is not always the best solution.

**The filter before anything ships:** Does this create clarity? Does this build confidence? Does this reflect excellence? If no, rewrite it.
