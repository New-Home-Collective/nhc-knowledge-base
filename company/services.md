## New Home Collective

A real estate team in Lexington and Central Kentucky, brokered by eXp Realty.

**Approved for use by Madison Feldman, September 14, 2026.** Nothing below
was invented. Each line comes from something the team already does in its
apps, skills, or brand file. Bob can revise it any time.

- **Buyer representation.** Helping people find and buy homes in Lexington
  and Central Kentucky.
- **Listing and selling.** Pricing, marketing, and selling homes. Home
  equity reports before listing, seller update reports while on the market.
- **Cash offers.** A cash offer path for sellers who want speed and
  certainty, run through the NHC Cash Offer Hub. Who the buyer is on an NHC
  cash offer, and whether BE's honesty rules apply, is not written down
  anywhere in this repo yet. Bob confirms.
- **Investor work.** Helping investors buy and sell in Central Kentucky.
- **Agent recruiting and development.** Bringing agents onto the team and
  training them. Recruiting content follows the "Agent recruiting" tone in
  the brand file.

## BE Property Ventures

BE Property Ventures LLC buys houses in Central Kentucky. All five, every
time the company is described:

- **Buy and hold.** Long term rentals in Central Kentucky.
- **Flip.** Buy it rough, fix it right, sell it.
- **Wholesale.** Get it under contract and bring in a partner to close when
  that is the better path.
- **Remodel.** Full renovation work on our own properties.
- **Build.** New construction.

Never describe BE as just a cash offer company. Never describe BE as just a
BRRRR operation. The range is the point. It means BE can say yes to more
situations than the guy who only does one thing.

BRRRR is one tool BE uses. It is not who BE is. BE is not a marketing company
that finds houses for someone else to buy. BE buys them.

The honesty rules around wholesaling live in the BE brand file, under Honesty
Guardrails. Read them before writing about wholesale.
