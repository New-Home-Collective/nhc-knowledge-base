## BE Property Ventures

BE Property Ventures LLC buys houses in Central Kentucky. All five, every
time the company is described:

- **Buy and hold.** Long term rentals in Central Kentucky.
- **Flip.** Buy it rough, fix it right, sell it.
- **Wholesale.** Get it under contract and bring in a partner to close when
  that is the better path.
- **Remodel.** Full renovation work on our own properties.
- **Build.** New construction.

Never describe BE as just a cash offer company. Never describe BE as just a
BRRRR operation. The range is the point. It means BE can say yes to more
situations than the guy who only does one thing.

BRRRR is one tool BE uses. It is not who BE is. BE is not a marketing company
that finds houses for someone else to buy. BE buys them.

The honesty rules around wholesaling live in the BE brand file, under Honesty
Guardrails. Read them before writing about wholesale.
