# The Question Bank

One per daily brief. Four to six per story time. Bank every answer in the Idea Bank with Bob's exact words. Check the Idea Bank before asking so nothing repeats.

The goal is scenes, not summaries. A good answer has a place, a person, a number, or an object in it. If the answer comes back general, ask one follow-up for the specific.

## Past

- Walk me through the day you decided to get your license. Where were you working, and what did the people around you say?
- What did a good night of tips at Bahama Breeze look like, and what did you do with the money?
- First deal you ever closed. Who was the client and what almost killed it?
- What did the lime green Impala sound like when it started? Where's the farthest you took it?
- What did Flint teach you that Lexington keeps proving right?
- Day 27, the morning after the case was dismissed. What was the first thing you did?
- What's a deal from the early years you'd never do again?
- When you and Eric started in 2012, what was the first property, and what did you get wrong on it?
- What did you believe about money at 25 that turned out to be dead wrong?
- Who gave you a shot when they didn't have to?

## Present

- What happened in this week's team meeting that nobody outside the room would expect?
- What's on your calendar this week that you're dreading, and why?
- What did a client say to you this week that stuck?
- What broke this week? Deal, system, AI agent, cabin, anything.
- What are you doing at the lake this weekend, actually?
- What's the current count toward 25 agents, and what's the honest blocker?
- Which of the six AI agents is closest to production, and what did it do wrong last?
- What's the hardest conversation you had this week?
- What did you spend money on this month that was worth it? What wasn't?
- What's something Blaire was right about lately?
- What did sobriety make easier this month? What does it still make harder?

## Future

- Describe the saltwater morning. The actual first hour of the day you're building toward.
- If NHC hits 250 agents, what does your Tuesday look like?
- What's the first thing you do when the passive income covers the bills?
- What do you want Bobby to know about work that school won't teach him?
- Which Lexington neighborhood do you think looks completely different in 10 years?
- What would make you walk away from all of it?
- What's the Italy trip with Blaire actually look like? First stop?
- Five years from now, what do you want a new agent to say NHC did for them?

## Follow-up moves

- "What did that cost you?" (turns a story into a lesson)
- "Who else was there?" (adds a human)
- "What's the number?" (adds proof)
- "What would you tell someone in that spot right now?" (turns memory into authority)

## Never ask

- Anything that fishes for a humble brag
- Anything about the case beyond what Bob has already shared publicly
- Compound questions. One thing at a time.
